Our Story

A Gathering Place for Food Lovers

Founded in 2018, Saffron & Ember began with a simple vision: bring together traditional open-fire cooking techniques and locally harvested organic produce.

Every dish on our menu tells a story of craftsmanship, balancing bold smoky aromas with delicate, fresh finishes. Whether you are celebrating a milestone or enjoying a casual dinner, our table is always set for you.
